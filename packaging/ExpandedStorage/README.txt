Expanded Storage 0.2.0
======================

[English]

Raises home Storage to 1000 slots (set 400-1000 in ExpandedStorage.ini).
Strongly recommended: Improved Inventory by anitosq (scrolling, search, filters)
https://www.nexusmods.com/mewgenics/mods/526

Requirements: Mewgenics Steam build 25143593 (1.1.21239), Mewjector, Mewtator.

Install: import this folder/ZIP in Mewtator, enable it, launch via Mewtator.
ExpandedStorage.log should say "Patched 3 capacity sites and the upgrade reward".

Settings (ExpandedStorage.ini):
  Capacity=1000   Storage size, 400-1000. Restart the game after changing it.
  Slots=All       All, or a list such as 2 or 1,3.

What changes in your save: the Storage upgrade level is raised to 17, the game's
own value for 400 slots, so the save keeps 400 slots even without this mod.
Butch's quests, donations and rewards are not changed.

IMPORTANT: whenever the mod is not active (disabled, no Mewtator, after a game
update, slot removed from Slots), Storage falls back to 400 slots and items
beyond 400 are moved to the Trash when you enter your home. Lowering Capacity
below your item count does the same. After a game update the mod shows a warning
and stays off. Before uninstalling, bring Storage down to 400 items or fewer.

Improved Inventory's 1024-item limit counts Storage and Trash together: with a
nearly full Storage, keep the Trash small.

[中文]

把家里的仓库提升到 1000 格（可在 ExpandedStorage.ini 中设为 400-1000）。
强烈推荐搭配 anitosq 的 Improved Inventory（滚动、搜索、筛选）：
https://www.nexusmods.com/mewgenics/mods/526

前置：Mewgenics Steam 版本号 25143593（1.1.21239）、Mewjector、Mewtator。

安装：在 Mewtator 中导入本文件夹或 ZIP，启用后通过 Mewtator 启动游戏。
ExpandedStorage.log 中应出现 "Patched 3 capacity sites and the upgrade reward"。

设置（ExpandedStorage.ini）：
  Capacity=1000   仓库格数，400-1000，修改后需重启游戏。
  Slots=All       All 表示全部槽位，也可以写 2 或 1,3。

对存档的改动：仓库升级等级被提升到 17，这是游戏自己表示 400 格的数值，
所以即使没有本模组，存档也保留 400 格。Butch 的任务、捐猫和奖励不受影响。

重要：只要模组没有生效（被禁用、不通过 Mewtator 启动、游戏更新后、或从 Slots
中去掉某个槽位），仓库就会回到 400 格，进入家园时超过 400 件的物品会被移入垃圾桶。
把 Capacity 调低到少于现有物品数时也一样。游戏更新后模组会弹窗提示并保持停用。
卸载前请先把仓库降到 400 件以内。

Improved Inventory 的 1024 件上限是仓库加垃圾桶合计：仓库接近满时请保持垃圾桶较空。

许可证：MIT。源码：https://github.com/sans2725/mewgenics-expanded-storage
